Lake Constance: Kesswil

May 2026 Village Note 1 min read 8/10 Part of: Central Europe Road Trip

Overview

Kesswil was a small stop on our drive along the southern shore of Lake Constance toward Konstanz. Calling it a city would be generous, but its traditional houses and preserved village atmosphere made it one of those places that rewards an unplanned pause.

There is no long sightseeing list here. Kesswil works as a short break for anyone following the same lakeside route, particularly if historic villages are part of the appeal of the drive. We continued from this side of Switzerland into Germany at Konstanz.
