Seferihisar

Apr 18, 2026 City Guide 2 min read 5/10 Part of: Seferihisar and Teos

Getting There

Seferihisar and Urla look very close on the map, but driving between them can be slower than expected. From the Çeşmealtı side of Urla, the journey to Seferihisar can approach an hour depending on traffic. Reaching either district separately from İzmir can sometimes feel easier than moving directly between the two.

Sığacık

Sığacık's center is still pleasant, but years of popularity have changed it. I preferred its older, more natural atmosphere. Souvenir shops, crowds and increasingly similar venues are pushing it toward a miniature version of Alaçatı.

The contrast with central Seferihisar is striking. The district center has far more apartment development than I expected from an Aegean town, while Sığacık presents the polished tourist image.

Crowds and Atmosphere

Seferihisar is very popular with people from İzmir, yet I have never fully warmed to it. Its venues and bays generally do not suit me. Weekends can be particularly difficult, when some day visitors bring noisy and aggressive behavior that changes the atmosphere completely.

The people who live here year-round and the regular summer residents are a different group. Day-trip pressure exists across many Aegean towns, but I have not encountered it this intensely in Urla.

Teos

Teos lies within the district near Sığacık and deserves its own note. The ancient city was less impressive than its history suggests, although the olive trees and rural setting gave the visit some character.

Verdict

There are individual places I enjoy, but Sığacık's loss of its old natural character and the weekend crowds keep creating distance between Seferihisar and me.
